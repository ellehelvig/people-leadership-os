# Sample issues for the fictional weekly brief

These are fictional issue records for Harbor. Issue identifiers, dates, quantities, and other numbers are illustrative. They are not actual GitHub issues and do not describe real people, companies, or internal processes.

## Illustrative issue #101: Decision request, AI-assisted People work

**Status:** Open  
**Owner:** People Operations lead  
**Decision needed:** Should Harbor allow limited AI-assisted drafting for non-sensitive People materials while tool guidance is reviewed?  
**Deadline:** Before the next People Leadership Team review, illustrative.  
**Options:** Allow bounded drafting with human review; pause all exploration; let each team choose its own approach.  
**Recommendation:** Allow bounded drafting only for non-sensitive materials. Do not use AI output to make or recommend an individual employee decision.  
**Consultation:** Relevant Harbor technology and control partners need to confirm boundaries.  
**Cost of delay:** Teams may continue to have different assumptions about what is allowed.  
**Source facts:** The tool and information boundaries have not been confirmed for this proposed use.
