# People Leadership Team operating rhythm

## Weekly: priorities and decisions

**Purpose:** Keep the People Leadership Team focused on the work that needs a decision, escalation, or cross-functional action this week.

**Participants:** Chief People Officer (CPO), People function leads, and a topic owner when their work is on the agenda.

**Before the meeting:** Issue owners update the status, decision needed, deadline, recommendation, and consulted partners on the relevant GitHub issue. The Chief of Staff prepares a short brief from open issues and flags missing owners or evidence.

**Who decides what:**

- The CPO decides matters within the CPO's delegated authority, including People priorities and unresolved trade-offs.
- A function lead decides within their area when the decision does not change an agreed People-wide priority or create a cross-functional commitment.
- The CPO identifies decisions that need a parent-company or other executive decision. The issue remains open until the decision owner and next step are clear.

**The meeting must produce:**

- A decision or a named decision owner and due date for each item requiring action.
- A clear priority change where work is added, delayed, or stopped.
- An updated issue with the decision, rationale, owner, and next step.
- A short list of asks for partners outside the People function.

## Monthly: operating health and alignment

**Purpose:** Check whether People work is progressing as intended and whether Harbor should align with or diverge from a Meridian Group program.

**Participants:** CPO and People function leads. Invite parent-company counterparts or cross-functional partners when a decision requires their input.

**Before the meeting:** Issue owners bring a brief status and evidence for work that is at risk, blocked, or ready for a decision. The Chief of Staff brings forward unresolved alignment questions and decisions that need a review.

**Who decides what:**

- The CPO sets or changes People priorities for Harbor.
- Function leads recommend changes to their operating plans and identify capacity constraints.
- The relevant parent-company decision owner decides matters that belong to Meridian Group. The CPO decides whether Harbor's recommendation is to adopt, adapt, or keep a separate approach.

**The meeting must produce:**

- A short record of the decisions made and the reasons for them.
- Updated owners and next steps for work that is delayed or blocked.
- A recorded alignment choice for each program reviewed, including any conditions or review date.
- A clear statement of which issue or decision record contains the lasting rationale.

## Quarterly: direction and resource choices

**Purpose:** Review the People agenda against Harbor's business direction and decide what to fund, change, or stop for the next planning period.

**Participants:** CPO and People function leads. Include the Harbor executive team or Meridian Group counterparts when their decisions or resources are needed.

**Before the meeting:** Function leads bring their recommended priorities, major dependencies, risks, and resource needs. The Chief of Staff summarizes open decisions and changes since the prior review. Evidence should be labeled as fact, estimate, or inference.

**Who decides what:**

- The CPO recommends the People agenda and decides within their authority.
- Harbor's executive decision owner resolves business trade-offs outside the CPO's authority.
- Meridian Group's decision owner resolves parent-level policy or resource questions.
- Function leads own execution plans after the priorities are agreed.

**The meeting must produce:**

- An agreed People priority set for the next planning period.
- A record of work to start, continue, defer, or stop, with an owner for each decision.
- Decisions or explicit asks for Harbor and Meridian Group leaders.
- Decision records for choices that are material, difficult to reverse, or likely to be revisited.

## Working rule

A meeting is not complete because discussion happened. The issue or decision record must show what was decided, who owns the next step, and when the team will check progress.
